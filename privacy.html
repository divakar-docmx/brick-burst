Brick Burst Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Brick Burst is a brick-breaker game for Android and the web. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play it. In short: Brick Burst does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

Information we collect

None. The game has no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking. The Android app doesn't request any permissions, including internet access.

Information stored on your device

To remember your progress, the game saves a few settings on your device:

  • your best score
  • the furthest level you reached and which levels you have cleared
  • your chosen ball speed

This information never leaves your device. If your Android device's backup feature is turned on, Android may include it in your device backup under your own Google account. You can erase it at any time by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

Sharing

Because no data is collected, none is shared with or sold to anyone.

Children

Brick Burst does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised on the project's GitHub page: github.com/divakar-docmx/brick-burst/issues.
